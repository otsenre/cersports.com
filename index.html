Under the hood

HOW THE MODEL WORKS

A committee of six independent AI pickers — cross-checked against our own statistical models — reads every slate each morning. One consensus output. No gut calls.

01
Data Scrape
Live game logs, rest days, back-to-backs, team ratings, injury context, and historical matchup data pulled fresh every morning via Playwright.
02
Rule Engine + ELO
A rule-based model scores every matchup on net rating, pace, fatigue index, and form. An ELO + XGBoost model trained on a decade of data produces win probability and predicted margin.
03
Consensus + Publish
Every picker's read is cross-referenced against the statistical models. Agreement scoring produces a unified CCR rating. Only plays above threshold qualify. Report published automatically with social cards by by 10:30 AM PT.
The Committee · six independent pickers, one card

Each picker reads the slate independently. We keep their identities under wraps — you get the consensus, not the noise.

Our rating system

WHAT IS CCR?

The CERSports Confidence Rating is a 0–10 score produced by our consensus engine. It measures how much edge the system has detected across the full six-picker committee combined.

9+
Elite
Full model agreement. Rare. Maximum edge detected.
7–8.9
Strong
Standard qualifier for posted picks. Strong consensus.
5–6.9
Solid
Solid data lean. Below our posting threshold.
<5
Noise
We don't post what the model doesn't believe in.
