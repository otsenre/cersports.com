Staking starts 2026-08-27. From that day wagers follow the bounce: the ledger's base for that pick, plus 1u after a loss and 2u at most, reset by a win. A push moves nothing and the next wager repeats.
Everything before that date keeps its flat stakes — the record is not restaked.
